{%- comment -%} A date every design can set its own way. .date-text is the plain date, one string, in the form `month` asks for: full December 8, 2026 (default) abbr Dec 8, 2026 .date-parts (a view-extra) is the same date in pieces, for a design that sets it differently: all three month forms, then the day and the year. .date-m-full December .date-m-abbr Dec .date-m-ap Dec. newspaper (AP) style: Jan. Feb. Aug. Sept. Oct. Nov. Dec., the rest spelled out .date-d 8 .date-y , 2026 A design that shows .date-parts hides .date-text and the month forms it doesn't want, and can set the day large or drop the year (an archive's year headings already say it). The datetime attribute always carries the full date. Usage: {% include date.html date=post.date class="card-date" month="abbr" %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign dt_i = include.date | date: "%-m" | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign dt_ap = "Jan.,Feb.,March,April,May,June,July,Aug.,Sept.,Oct.,Nov.,Dec." | split: "," -%} {%- if include.month == "abbr" %}{% assign dt_fmt = "%b %-d, %Y" %}{% else %}{% assign dt_fmt = "%B %-d, %Y" %}{% endif -%} {{ include.date | date: dt_fmt }}{{ include.date | date: "%B" }}{{ include.date | date: "%b" }}{{ dt_ap[dt_i] }} {{ include.date | date: "%-d" }}, {{ include.date | date: "%Y" }}
