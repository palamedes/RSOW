{%- comment -%} Shared post card: the home page, /archive/ and /about/, in every design. The narration chip is a real button on the date row, so the card can no longer be one big (a button nested in an anchor is invalid, and its clicks would navigate). Instead the title carries the link and .card-link::after stretches over the whole card, which keeps the entire card clickable while leaving the chip — lifted above it — free to handle its own clicks. The filter/search data lives on the wrapper because the archive script shows and hides that element. One card for every design (see _data/views.yml): it carries everything any design shows, in Default's order; a design hides what it doesn't use and reorders the rest (.card-figure and .card-body can be display:contents). .card-figure wraps the thumbnail (display:contents in Default) .card-kicker the first topic, a kicker or a sticker (view-extra) .card-byline "By Jason Ellis" (view-extra) .card-date _includes/date.html: "December 8, 2026", plus the date in pieces (.date-parts, a view-extra) .card-excerpt-more words 26-64 of the excerpt, inside .card-excerpt just before the "..."
