{{ m.title }} Designer

Color a design: upload a silhouette, pick colors from the {{ m.title | downcase }} tests, and click the parts you want in each. Or make a test pattern: pick the shades you want to try and download a LightBurn file with a labelled square for each. The preview shows roughly how it will come out.

It all runs in your browser. Your image never leaves this page.

Pick the shades to test

Choose a color on the right, then click its shades to add them. Each becomes a square with its settings engraved underneath, ready to run from LightBurn.

Drop a silhouette here

A black-and-white image works best: PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG.

Colors

Pick a color, then a shade, then click areas of the design. Order the shades by color or by speed to find the fastest. Settings that melt {{ m.title | downcase }} aren't offered.

Test pattern

Click shades above to add or remove them. Up to 29: LightBurn allows 30 layers, and the labels use one.
