A pup with a heart on its tag looks up adoringly, all inside a white ring. DOG goes big in gold, and a gold paw print signs off underneath.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.

It goes with [I Like Dogs More Than People](/tinkered-tactile/work/dogs-more-than-people-coaster/), for the days that one's more honest. We can add your dog's name.
