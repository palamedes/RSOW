MOM goes big and gold in the middle. Around the ring are her job titles: first friend, gooddest (half goodest, half goddess), mother, teacher, generous, best chef, merry and delightful.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.

There's a [Best Dad in the Galaxy](/tinkered-tactile/work/best-dad-coaster/) to go with it, so the two make a set. It works for Nana or Grandma too: tell us the words that fit yours and we'll put them around the ring.
