Each one gets the drink's name in gold script, a drawing of the glass with its garnish, the recipe, and a short tasting note at the bottom. The Amaretto Sour's is "Sweet, nutty, a little tart." The Mai Tai's says "Paradise is closer than you think."

Pictured: Vodka & Cranberry, Tom Collins, Sex on the Beach, Mai Tai, Amaretto Sour and Dirty Shirley. That's a sample, not the whole menu. We make these for pretty much any drink, so if yours isn't here, tell us what it is. Your house cocktail works too, or the one your bar is known for.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.
