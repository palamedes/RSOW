This one was designed for Sadie. The name is on the walnut lid, the name and the years are inside it, and a paw print is pressed into each leather band. None of it shouts. The idea was a piece that still looks right on a shelf ten years from now.

The body is spalted maple, turned between a walnut top and base, with the leather inlaid flush and laced with a cross stitch.
