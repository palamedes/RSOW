New Bern was settled in 1710 by Swiss and German colonists and named for Bern, Switzerland, whose symbol is the bear. The bear stayed. This one is a forest of pines with geese flying over, and the town's name in gold on the banner.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.

Every town has its version: a lighthouse, a mascot, a bridge everyone complains about. Tell us yours.
