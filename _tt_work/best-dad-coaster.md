DAD goes big and gold in the middle. Around the ring: the man, the myth, the legend, and below that his day jobs, husband, father, protector and hero.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.

It's the other half of the [Best Mom in the Galaxy](/tinkered-tactile/work/best-mom-coaster/) coaster, so the two make a set. It works for Grandpa or Pop too: tell us the words that fit him and we'll put them around the ring.
