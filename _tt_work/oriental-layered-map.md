Oriental sits on the Neuse River just short of Pamlico Sound and calls itself the Sailing Capital of North Carolina, which makes it a good map: more water than land, and a town that hugs the edges.

Each layer is cut from its own sheet. The top one carries the land, with every street engraved into it. The five below are water, each a little deeper and darker than the last, cut from real depth soundings so the shading actually means something. Stack them flush and the harbor falls away into the river.
