One coaster for each branch. The emblems go in white, and the lettering and stars in gold. The rough, hand-split edge of the slate stays the way it came.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.

Get one branch, a set of four, or the whole lineup for a family that has a little of everything. Add a name, rank or years of service and it's a retirement gift, a homecoming present or the start of a wall at the VFW.
