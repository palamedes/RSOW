Both are drawn like an old soda-fountain ad. The Coca-Cola has an old pickup and a drugstore behind the bottle and the glass, with bottle caps piled in front. The Pepsi has a delivery van coming down the road to a soda shop. Each one's name goes in gold at the top, and ALWAYS REFRESHING runs across a ribbon at the bottom.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.

They're sold one at a time, so you can get one of each and nobody at the table has to pick a side.
