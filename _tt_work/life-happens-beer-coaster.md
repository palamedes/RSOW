LIFE HAPPENS... arcs across the top, Beer goes big in gold script, and HELPS sits underneath in white, with a gold star and flourishes to finish it.

Every bit of it is laser engraved. There's no paint: the white and the gold both come from the laser changing the stone itself, so there's nothing to wash off. We don't seal or coat them either, so there's no finish to bubble or peel. The bare slate soaks up what drips off a sweating glass.

It's the after-work half of [Life Happens, Coffee Helps](/tinkered-tactile/work/life-happens-coffee-coaster/): one for the morning, one for the evening. We can make it wine, bourbon or whatever helps at your house.
