I must make -- and I don't mean I have to go to the bathroom really badly either.  Rather, I mean that I have a need to create things.

Some people are inherently creative and/or craft-sy.  I'm one of those people.

I have often wondered why some folks have a very strong desire, almost compunction to create things.. and some don't.  I can't really explain it, but it seems an interesting phenomenon.

I think for me it has a lot to do with what I do for a living.  I'm a software engineer --  As such, I produce nothing physical.  I twiddle bits and massage pixels for a living -- not very fullfilling for a person that has a need to produce.
