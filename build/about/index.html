About

Hi, I'm Jason.

A calm, considerate problem solver who has been writing software professionally since 1997. I'm self-taught, I've led teams and companies, and I still love the moment something I built starts working.

Full résumé · LinkedIn · GitHub

The short version

Today I'm Head of Development at Ambiki, an EMR built for pediatric speech, occupational and physical therapists, and a senior engineer at its sister company, Sidekick Therapy Partners. Before that I co-founded and ran engineering at a web shop, architected the web interface for an IBM product, and built and sold my own company in 2000.

My path hasn't been a straight line. I learned discipline and Korean as an Army signals intelligence analyst, and I've twice stepped away from software to work as a police officer and sheriff's deputy. Each time I came back to building things, which is the part I can't stay away from.

What that adds up to for you: someone who has seen how software succeeds and fails across a quarter century, who stays calm when things go sideways, and who will tell you the truth about your project, including when the right answer is not to build it.

How I build today

Ruby on Rails and PostgreSQL for most applications, Hotwire and modern JavaScript on the front end, static sites when a server isn't needed, and boring, inexpensive infrastructure. I use modern AI tools every day. I'm faster and more thorough for it, and every line still gets my review.
