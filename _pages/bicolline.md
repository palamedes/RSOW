## Bicolline

### Colibrille and The Horse and Hound

Bicolline is an immersive medieval fantasy live-action role-playing (LARP) event held in Quebec, Canada, where thousands of participants gather in a sprawling, hand-built village to role-play, battle, trade, and live out elaborate storylines over several days. The event is known for its scale, player-constructed structures, in-game economy, politics, and social elements, including field battles and community activities.
